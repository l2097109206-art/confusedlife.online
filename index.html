How "speaking your mind" works here

You write it, not your name. On The Clarity Wall you share what's weighing on you in your own words. There's no profile, no sign-up, and an email is optional and only used if you ask us to. Your post is just a voice on a wall.

We don't keep anything about you. We don't run ad trackers, we don't build a profile, and posting isn't linked to an identity. The only thing stored is the anonymous words you chose to share — and you can ask us to take them down anytime.

Others meet you there. People who've felt the same can send a hug, say "me too," or leave a few kind words. Nobody is performing expertise — it's just humans recognising each other. You'll also get a short, non-clinical reflection back to help you think it through.
