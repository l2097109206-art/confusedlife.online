How we work

We don't sell you a transformation. ConfusedLife.online is an independent editorial project. There's no course, no coaching funnel, and no 5am routine waiting at the end of an article. When we recommend something, it's because it's cheap, boring and likely to work.

We cite things that exist. Every guide links to the research or the original source it leans on. Where the evidence is thin — and on questions of meaning, it often is — we say so instead of dressing an opinion up as science.

We're not clinicians. We write about ordinary confusion: the career wobble, the Sunday-night dread, the sense that everyone else got a script you never received. That's different from a mental health condition, and if what you're experiencing looks more like the latter, the most useful thing on this site is our recommendation to talk to someone qualified.
